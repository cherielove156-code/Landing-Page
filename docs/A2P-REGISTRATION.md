## Campaign

**Use case:** choose **Mixed** if it is offered (it covers reminders and marketing in one campaign). If you must pick one, choose **Marketing**.

**Campaign description:**

> Rie Artistry LLC sends text messages to beauty professionals who opt in on our website. Messages include reminders and joining details for free live online trainings they registered for and, only for people who separately opt in, promotional messages about Rie Artistry programs and offers.

**How people opt in (message flow / call to action):**

> People opt in on our web registration form at https://training.rieartistry.com/free-training-page. After entering their first name, email, mobile number, and industry, they may check either or both of two optional, unchecked boxes: "Text me reminders about this free training." and "Text me offers and updates from Rie Artistry." Each box has its own disclosure that names Rie Artistry, states the type of messages, says message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help, and that consent is not required to register or a condition of purchase, with links to our Privacy Policy (https://training.rieartistry.com/privacy-policy) and Terms (https://training.rieartistry.com/terms). Texting is optional. People can register without checking either box.

## Sample messages

Every sample starts with the brand name and ends with opt-out language. Use full links on your own domain in real messages; carriers often reject shortened links such as bit.ly.

1. > Rie Artistry: Hi {first name}, you're registered for the free live training on Sun, Oct 18 at 7 PM ET. We'll text your joining link before we start. Reply STOP to opt out.
2. > Rie Artistry: Reminder, the free training starts in 1 hour at 7 PM ET. Join here: https://training.rieartistry.com/free-training-page Reply STOP to opt out.
3. > Rie Artistry: Enrollment is open for our 8-week group mentorship for beauty professionals. Details: https://training.rieartistry.com/free-training-page Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

## Keywords and automatic replies

| | Keywords | Reply message |
|---|---|---|
| Opt-in | START, YES | Rie Artistry: You're subscribed to text messages. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. |
| Opt-out | STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT | Rie Artistry: You've been unsubscribed and will receive no further texts. Reply START to resubscribe. |
| Help | HELP, INFO | Rie Artistry: For help, email rieartistrystudio@gmail.com. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. |

HighLevel handles STOP automatically and marks the contact as Do Not Disturb for texts. STOP turns off all texts from your number, both reminders and promotions, which is how the Terms describe it.

## Check before you submit

- [ ] The landing page is published at a public address, and the reviewer can see the form and both checkboxes. A page that says "Preview mode" may be questioned, so publish the live version or be ready to explain.
- [ ] Privacy Policy and Terms open at their addresses and include the text-message sections.
- [ ] The Privacy Policy includes: "No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes."
- [ ] Legal name and address match your EIN records.

## Timing

Approval often takes several days and can take a few weeks. The training is on October 18, so submit as soon as the landing page is live. Until the campaign is approved, plan to send reminders by email only.
